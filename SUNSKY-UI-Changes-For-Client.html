SUNSKY website: what has been updated

Prepared 20 October 2026  ·  All items below are live on the website now

What this document is. A list of the 21 improvements to the website that are finished and online, written so you can check each one yourself. Under every item there is a short line telling you where to look.

At the end there is a short list of the 5 items that are not finished, and the reason for each. None of those are waiting on us.

Finished and live 21

1. Dates you cannot fly are now greyed out

When you search for a flight and hotel together, the calendar greys out any day that has no possible return flight, so you cannot pick a holiday that cannot be flown. If we are not certain about a day, it stays available rather than being hidden.

How to check: Search a flight and hotel to any destination and open the calendar. Some days are grey and cannot be clicked.

2. The calendar opens on the first month that has a flight

If you have not chosen a date yet, the calendar jumps straight to the first month where flying is possible and tells you the earliest date. It never picks a date for you.

How to check: Search a destination that has only a few flights. The calendar opens on that month instead of on today.

3. Hotel prices use the cheapest of the airports you chose

The departure airports you tick on the results page now carry over to the hotel page. Each date shows the cheapest complete package from any of those airports, and tells you which airport that price is from. Before, the hotel page used only one airport for every date.

How to check: Tick Brussels and Amsterdam, then open a hotel. One date can be from Brussels and the next from Amsterdam, whichever is cheaper.

4. Room prices compare against the room you picked

The room you have chosen says "Selected". Every other room shows how much more or less it costs per person compared with your choice. Before, everything was compared with the cheapest room and a cheaper option never showed a minus.

How to check: On a hotel page, pick a more expensive room. The cheaper rooms now show a minus amount.

5. Destinations you cannot reach are greyed out

If you choose Charleroi as your departure airport, destinations with no flight from Charleroi are greyed out and say so. It also works the other way round. A country stays available if at least one place in it can be reached.

How to check: Choose Charleroi, then open the destination list.

6. Only trip lengths that really exist can be chosen

The trip length buttons can only be clicked when a real, complete package exists for that number of days. A length you chose earlier stays visible and marked as not available rather than disappearing.

How to check: Pick a date on a hotel page. Some of the length buttons cannot be clicked.

7. The result count says "holidays" and shows when it is still counting

The page now says "3,842 holidays found", or "624 holidays in Turkey" when one destination is chosen. While the total is still being worked out it says so, instead of showing a number that is only a guess.

How to check: Change a filter on the results page and watch the number above the list.

8. Short trips offer their dates instead of a misleading price

A "from" price is only shown for trips of at least 3 days for a city trip, or 5 days otherwise. A hotel that only has shorter trips stays in the list and offers "View available dates" instead of a price that cannot be compared with the others.

How to check: Look for a hotel showing "View available dates" rather than a price.

9. The same wording everywhere

The site now uses one fixed set of words: "Check price", "Not available", "Price temporarily unavailable". Before, different pages used different words for the same situation, and a slow supplier could be shown as "sold out" when it had simply not answered yet.

How to check: Compare the wording on the results page and the hotel page. They match.

10. One flight routing switch instead of three options

There is now a single setting with two positions: all permitted flights, or direct outbound and return only. Turning it off goes back to all permitted routes and can never offer a route the destination does not allow.

How to check: On the results page, switch it on and off. The count and the prices update.

11. Number of hotels shown next to each destination

The destination filter shows how many hotels each place has, for example "Turkey 624, Spain 811". A hotel is counted once even if you have selected both the country and the city.

How to check: Open the destination filter on the results page.

12. Flight-only search is now instant

Flight results come from our own stored data in a fraction of a second, and we only contact the airline once, at the moment the customer presses Select. The customer is told before booking if the price has changed or the flight has gone.

How to check: Use the Flights search. Results appear almost immediately.

13. Sorting no longer puts unrated hotels first

Sorting by stars from 1 to 5 used to begin with every hotel that has no star rating, which made the list look like it was full of bad hotels. Hotels without a rating now come last whichever direction you sort in. The order also stays the same every time you reload instead of shuffling.

How to check: Sort by "Stars: 1 to 5". Rated hotels come first, unrated ones at the end.

14. Popular departure airports appear first

The airports you mark as Popular in the dashboard now appear at the top of every departure airport list. This changes the order only. It never changes a price, and the cheapest package can still depart from an airport that is not marked popular.

How to check: Mark an airport Popular in the dashboard, then open a departure airport list on the website.

15. Category pages are for navigation, not prices

Zonvakanties, Stedentrips, All Inclusive and Last Minute now open a page listing the places they cover, with no prices on it. Choosing a place keeps the category and adds that place. "Toon alles" keeps the category and shows everything in it.

How to check: Click Stedentrips on the homepage, then Barcelona. The results stay within Stedentrips.

16. Last Minute and All Inclusive now behave correctly

Last Minute starts with a travel period of today up to 42 days ahead, shown in the search summary, which the customer can change. All Inclusive now keeps only offers whose actual room rate is all inclusive, rather than hotels that merely carry the label.

How to check: Click Last Minute on the homepage. The dates are already filled in and can be changed.

17. A homepage card can carry its own travel period

If you set a start and end date on a card in the dashboard, that period is applied to the start of the trip. As soon as the customer changes the dates themselves, their choice replaces it.

How to check: Set a date range on a card in the dashboard and click that card on the website.

18. A customer's own choice is never replaced silently

What the customer chose always takes priority over anything the site filled in automatically. Changing one thing changes only that thing. Switching between hotel only and flight plus hotel keeps the destination, travellers, dates and meal plan.

How to check: Search a flight and hotel, then switch to hotel only. Your destination, dates and travellers stay.

19. The filter list has been cleaned up

"Soort vakantie" and "Soort kamer" have been removed. Themes now include Adults Only and Family. The star filter now includes hotels with no official star classification as their own option, instead of treating them as missing data.

How to check: Open the filters on the results page.

20. The alternative flights panel is simpler

The flight you already have is shown separately and is no longer repeated in the list below. Flights load 20 at a time. Each one shows how much more or less it costs per person against your complete package including the hotel, not just against the flight on its own.

How to check: On a hotel page, click "Kies andere vlucht".

21. "Calculating" instead of a price for the wrong party

Prices are prepared in advance only for 2 adults in 1 room. For any other party, for example 3 adults or a family with children, the price is worked out on request and the page says it is calculating. It will never show a 2 adult price to someone searching for 3.

How to check: Search for 3 adults. The cards say they are calculating before the prices appear.

Not finished yet 5

None of these are waiting on website work. Four need something from the supplier side first, and one belongs in the dashboard rather than the website.
